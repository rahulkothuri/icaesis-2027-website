Call for Papers

Submit your research for ICAESIS-2027

The ICAESIS-2027 invites original research papers from students, researchers, academicians, and industry professionals. The conference accepts paper submissions on the following tracks and topics (but not limited to):



1. Innovative Sustainable Energy & Power Systems

Focuses on cutting-edge technologies and strategies that accelerate the transition toward clean, reliable, and sustainable energy.

  • Renewable energy systems: solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, and hybrid solutions
  • Smart grids, microgrids, and distributed energy resources (DERs)
  • Energy storage systems: batteries, hydrogen, supercapacitors
  • Grid integration, grid resilience, protection, and power quality
  • Digital twins and AI-driven energy forecasting
  • Electric vehicles, charging infrastructure, and vehicle-to-grid (V2G/G2V) systems
  • High-voltage engineering and advanced monitoring techniques

2. Next-Gen Power Electronics, Embedded Systems & VLSI for Sustainability

Highlights advancements in power electronic converters, intelligent embedded systems, and VLSI technologies focused on high efficiency and sustainability.

  • High-efficiency converters using wide bandgap semiconductors (GaN, SiC)
  • Multilevel inverters and advanced modulation/control strategies
  • Power electronics for EVs, renewables, and industrial applications
  • Embedded architectures for low-power intelligent devices
  • FPGA, DSP, and microcontroller-based control systems
  • VLSI/ASIC design for energy-efficient computing
  • IoT-enabled power electronic systems and cyber-physical integration

3. Intelligent Systems: AI, IoT & Cybersecurity for Sustainability

Explores intelligent, interconnected, and secure systems that enable sustainable and resilient infrastructures.

  • AI/ML for engineering optimisation and smart decision-making
  • IoT systems for smart cities, smart grids, and digital ecosystems
  • 5G/6G communication technologies for sustainable connectivity
  • Robotics, autonomous systems, and intelligent sensing
  • Cybersecurity for critical infrastructure and industrial systems
  • Blockchain for sustainable operations and energy trading
  • Edge computing and real-time analytics for sustainable applications

4. Sustainable Automation, Manufacturing & Industrial Innovation

Emphasizes sustainable and efficient industrial processes through modern automation and manufacturing advancements.

  • Industry 4.0/5.0 technologies and sustainable digital factories
  • Smart automation systems and intelligent control
  • Robotics, cobots, and mechatronic innovations
  • Additive manufacturing (3D/4D printing) for engineering solutions
  • Energy-efficient manufacturing and green production practices
  • Predictive maintenance, digital twins, and condition monitoring
  • Mechatronics and servo systems for industrial sustainability

5. Innovative Materials, Biotechnology & Healthcare for Sustainability

Focuses on transformative materials, biotechnologies, and healthcare innovations contributing to sustainable engineering.

  • Functional and advanced materials for energy, electronics, and the environment
  • Sustainable materials: biodegradable, recyclable, and circular-economy based
  • Nanomaterials and nano-enabled devices
  • Biomedical devices, wearables, and smart healthcare systems
  • Biotechnology for water treatment, waste management, and bioenergy
  • Materials for EVs, aerospace, and next-generation infrastructure
  • Sustainable construction materials and smart building technologies

Submission Guidelines

  • The paper must be original and it should not be submitted elsewhere from the submission to publication.
  • The organization of the paper is as follows: Abstract, Introduction, Literature Survey, Methodology, Discussion and Conclusion.
  • Papers with high plagiarism will be rejected without revision.
  • Papers that do not comply with the IEEE template or number of pages will be rejected without revision.
  • Authors of accepted papers cannot be changed.
  • Accepted papers should be revised according to the review comments in order to proceed to the further registration step.
  • Non-refundable registration fee must be paid prior to uploading the final IEEE formatted, camera-ready version of the paper and e-copyright form.
  • All submitted manuscripts will be subjected to the review process. Accepted & presented papers will be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore, subject to meeting IEEE Xplore's scope and quality requirements.
  • Non-presented papers will not be submitted to IEEE Xplore digital library as per IEEE no-show policy.
